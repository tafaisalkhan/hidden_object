Privacy Policy

Game: Hidden Object Adventure • Effective Date: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Hidden Object Adventure collects, uses, and safeguards user information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (including GDPR & COPPA).

1. Information Collection & Use

We respect your privacy. Hidden Object Adventure is designed as an offline-first puzzle game. We do not require users to create an account or provide direct personal information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number).

However, the app utilizes standard third-party services that may automatically collect certain diagnostic and non-personal technical information to provide advertisements, in-app purchases, and performance analytics.

2. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with third-party service providers that have their own privacy policies governing data handling:

  • Google Play Services: Used for platform distribution, license verification, and app security.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID) and device information to deliver relevant ads.
    How Google uses information from sites or apps
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Used securely for optional digital in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads"). All payment transactions and credit card data are processed directly by Google Play Store servers and are never accessed or stored by our app.
    Google Play Terms of Service

3. Log Data and Analytics

In case of an unexpected error or crash in the game, diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system version, app state at the time of crash) may be collected anonymously via Google Play Console crash diagnostics to help us diagnose bugs and improve game stability.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is designed for all audiences and puzzle enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy, our ad requests are configured to respect privacy guidelines.

5. Data Security

We value your trust. Any game save progress (such as unlocked sublevels and star scores) is saved locally on your device storage and is not transmitted to external private servers.

6. User Choices & Ad Opt-Out

You can opt out of personalized advertisements or reset your Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads and select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in our app or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
