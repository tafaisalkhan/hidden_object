This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Hidden Object Adventure (available on Google Play) published by tafaisalkhan. 1. Overview & Offline-First Architecture

We respect your personal privacy. Hidden Object Adventure is designed as a standalone, offline-playable hidden object puzzle game. We do not require you to register an account, log in with social media, or provide personal identity details (such as your real name, home address, or phone number) to play our game.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)

Our game is family-friendly and designed for puzzle lovers of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. In accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy, all ad requests are configured with appropriate family and age-appropriate content ratings.

5. Local Data Storage & Progress Retention

Your game save data (such as completed levels, earned 3-star ratings, high scores, and sound/music settings) is stored locally on your device storage. We do not upload your gameplay progress to private external servers.

6. User Control & How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You have full control over advertising identifiers on your Android device. You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Because our game stores gameplay progress locally on your device and does not maintain remote user accounts, you can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the game's storage cache under Settings → Apps → Hidden Object Adventure → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new legal requirements, or Google Play policy modifications. Changes will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
