## 5. Local Data Storage & Offline Gameplay
All gameplay save data (unlocked levels, 3-star ratings, high scores, and audio preferences) is stored **locally on your device**. We do not upload your save game files or progress to external private servers.

---

## 6. How to Opt-Out or Reset Advertising Identifiers
Android users have complete control over personalized advertising. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time:
1. Open your Android device **Settings**.
2. Tap **Google** ➔ **Ads** (or **Privacy** ➔ **Ads**).
3. Select **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.

---

## 7. Data Deletion Requests
Because all game progress is stored locally on your device and no remote accounts are maintained, you can delete all data at any time by clearing the app data:
- Go to **Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Hidden Object Adventure ➔ Storage ➔ Clear Storage / Clear Data**, or simply uninstall the game.
